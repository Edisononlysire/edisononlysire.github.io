RESEARCH DIRECTION

Research trajectory

Biological systems provide the starting point for studying complex systems, with the research later extending to long-horizon agent decisions. The central question is how to build a reliable situation picture, compare consequences, and keep machine recommendations open to human review and correction.

Unifying research question

Represent state → project intervention → inspect mechanisms → maintain tasks → acquire evidence → support human–AI command. Each stage connects a concrete project, a testable method question, and a defined contribution. The destination is collaborative decisions under human objectives and authority.

  1. 01

    Observation to situation · OmniCell / GOformer / SPARSE

    Make heterogeneous observations comparable

    Question: If a model confuses a cell’s molecular identity with its spatial surroundings, later forecasting and decisions start from an unreliable state.

    Work: OmniCell models genes, cells, and tissue topology together. GOformer tests how cell, gene, and GO-function representations are read out. SPARSE adds coordinate-aware, multi-resolution spatial adaptation to frozen transcriptomic features while masked expression reconstruction protects molecular semantics.

    Contribution: Built OmniCell baselines and performed data analysis and evaluation; designed GOformer’s separate protocols for a shared linear probe and gene-plus-GO attention fusion; led nearly all of SPARSE, from research question and architecture through experiments, ablations, and writing.

    Research link: These projects establish what the system looks like now. Command decisions also need to anticipate what an intervention changes.

  2. 02

    Situation to consequences · DynPerturb

    Project effects across time, intensity, and space

    Question: The same intervention can have different effects at different times, strengths, and locations. A static representation cannot identify when or where to act.

    Work: DynPerturb joins temporal encoding, spatial adjacency, and memory to study perturbation propagation, regulatory rewiring, feedback, and candidate intervention windows.

    Contribution: Data analysis and model evaluation, ablation experiments, result organization, and manuscript preparation and revision, as specified in the paper. The analyses examined sensitivity to temporal resolution, feature selection, and sample conditions.

    Research link: Forecasts of possible consequences still need mechanisms and independent evidence before they can support consequential decisions.

  3. 03

    Predictions to mechanisms · BiOmics / Discovery

    Turn a judgment into an auditable evidence chain

    Question: Correlations and model scores alone cannot justify a high-impact judgment. We need to know its sources, reasoning path, and remaining gaps.

    Work: BiOmics connects explicit knowledge-graph relations, latent representations, and specialist tools through a retrieve–reason–predict workflow. BiOmics Discovery separates candidate generation from validation: freeze candidates first, then seek independent support, contradictory evidence, and missing tests.

    Contribution: Designed the BiOmics research idea and overall framework, including knowledge–tool–agent coordination and reasoning traceability; led Discovery’s question decomposition, candidate-freezing rule, evidence grading, and validation path.

    Research link: Sourced judgments must remain valid through long tasks and be revised when new evidence arrives.

  4. 04

    Single judgments to long tasks · EvoState / Legal Agent

    Maintain state as actions change the task

    Question: Old facts can become invalid, requirements can be resolved or reopened, and exact IDs and paths must survive. Actions are also constrained by authority and rules.

    Work: EvoState separates Current Trace, Event Memory, and Task State. A multi-operation editor revises them from observations, while a dependency-aware assembler preserves exact references for the next action. The Legal Agent internship translates actors, conditions, duties, and exceptions into computable task constraints.

    Contribution: Helped frame EvoState’s research idea, three-part state, update mechanism, and evaluation; focused on structured legal language and traceable reasoning workflows in the legal work.

    Research link: Once task state and authority boundaries are explicit, the system can decide what evidence is missing and whether the next action is permitted.

  5. 05

    Passive judgment to active evidence · AEP-ECRG

    Choose the next step under uncertainty and adversarial trust

    Question: Acting with weak evidence risks overreaction, while endless investigation consumes time and resources. A previously trusted source may also change behavior.

    Work: AEP-ECRG takes public observations, evidence, trust state, and budget as inputs to ask whether to investigate, what to inspect, and when to respond. Planned AEP learns candidate action value; ECRG constrains high-impact actions using evidence prerequisites and risk boundaries.

    Contribution: Formulated the problem and overall framework linking public evidence, belief updates, active acquisition, and runtime governance; defined comparisons of cost, benefit, and risk. The new training protocol and governance gains remain under evaluation.

    Research link: The machine now presents grounds, gaps, value of further investigation, and action risk as reviewable options.

  6. 06

    Long-term research direction

    Human–AI collaborative command

    Goal: Build a shared situational and decision interface for people and machines, with human authority over consequential actions.

    Machine supplies: Integrated observations, projected consequences, mechanisms and provenance, current task state, and the cost and risk of investigation or action. Recommendations expose source, validity, conflict, and missing evidence.

    People decide: Set objectives, authority, and risk limits; question evidence; approve, revise, or reject key actions. New observations and human feedback update the next state.

    Research agenda: The current projects supply representation, projection, explanation, memory, and evidence-acquisition components. The next step is to test them together in an auditable, correctable human–AI workflow.

RESEARCH WORK

Papers & ongoing projects

The projects below follow the research path above. Submission and exploratory statuses reflect information supplied in October 2026. Main paper figures are shown only for the two ICLR papers approved for display.

State representation · sole first author

SPARSE

ICLR 2027 · under review

Semantic-Preserving Adapter for Robust Spatial Embeddings

Research: Adds a lightweight spatial adapter to a frozen transcriptomic model. It builds a multi-resolution token hierarchy around each cell neighborhood, then combines coordinate propagation, spatial position encoding, cross-resolution attention, and top-down fusion. Masked expression reconstruction helps preserve molecular meaning.

Contribution: Led nearly the full project: framing the research question, designing the method, driving implementation, experiments and ablations, and organizing the paper and revisions.
